STEP 2: MARKET ANALYSIS (Per Index)
-----------------------------------
For each symbol (SPX500, GER30, FRA40, US30):

  a) STRUCTURE ANALYSIS (1M Chart with 5-candle lookback)
     • Identify swing highs/lows (~10 minute swings)
     • 2x BOS in same direction confirms trend
     • All analysis done on 1-minute candles

  b) LIQUIDITY SWEEP DETECTION
     • Monitor for price sweeping above previous swing high (sell setup)
     • Monitor for price sweeping below previous swing low (buy setup)
     • Confirm liquidity grab with wick/rejection

  c) ORDER BLOCK IDENTIFICATION
     • After liquidity sweep, identify the last opposite candle
     • For SELL: Last bullish candle before the sweep high
     • For BUY: Last bearish candle before the sweep low
     • Order block zone = Open to Close of that candle

STEP 3: ENTRY SIGNAL
--------------------
  • Wait for price to return to the Order Block zone
  • Entry triggered when price touches the OB level
  • Random 1-2 second delay added before order execution (anti-detection)

STEP 4: POSITION SIZING
-----------------------
  • Calculate risk based on adaptive settings:
    - Default: 1.5% of account balance
    - Reduced: 1.0% when daily drawdown reaches 2%
    - Stop: No new trades when daily drawdown hits 3%

  • Position Size Formula:
    Lot Size = (Account Balance × Risk%) / (Stop Loss Points × Point Value)

STEP 5: ORDER EXECUTION
-----------------------
  • Order Type: Market order
  • Stop Loss: Below/above the Order Block (1:2 RR minimum)
  • Take Profit: 2× the stop loss distance (2R target)
  • Random delay: 1-2 seconds before execution

STEP 6: TRADE MANAGEMENT
------------------------
  • No manual intervention - let trade hit TP or SL
  • Drawdown protection monitors account continuously
  • Telegram notifications sent for:
    - Trade entries
    - Trade exits (win/loss)
    - Daily summaries
    - Drawdown warnings

STEP 7: DAILY OPERATIONS
------------------------

  • News Filter: Avoids trading 30 min before/after high-impact news
  • Daily reset at midnight (drawdown tracking resets)
  • Heartbeat file updated every 60 seconds

STEP 8: END OF DAY
------------------

  • Force close times (local market time):
    - SPX500/US30: 3:45 PM ET (closes 4:00 PM)
    - GER30/FRA40: 5:15 PM CET (closes 5:30 PM)
  • No overnight positions held - prevents gap risk
  • Trade journal updated with daily summary
