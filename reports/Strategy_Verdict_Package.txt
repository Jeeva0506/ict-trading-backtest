CORE LOGIC:
  1. SETUP: Build 15-minute Opening Range Box (ORB) at market open
  2. SIGNAL: Wait for "displacement candle" that breaks ORB boundary
     - Must have 70%+ body ratio (strong candle, not a wick)
     - Must close in top/bottom 25% of range (commitment)
     - Must break ORB by 15%+ margin (not weak poke)
     - Must have real body > 0.5× ORB range
  3. ENTRY: Enter at close of displacement candle (market order)
     - Entry window: first 2-3 hours of session only
     - Max 60-minute breakout window after ORB forms
  4. FILTERS:
     - HTF Trend: H1 EMA20/50 alignment required (no counter-trend)
     - Min R: 3.0 for SPX500/NDX100, 5.0 for US30/US2000/GER30/FRA40
     - Max 2 trades per day, max 2 open positions
     - Spread guard: reject if spread > 30% of risk distance
     - News filter: skip 5 min before / 15 min after high-impact news
  5. EXIT (3-stage):
     - Stage 1: Raw SL at ORB opposite boundary + buffer
     - Stage 2: At 1.0R favorable, move SL to breakeven (entry price)
     - Stage 3: Trail with 0.5R buffer (SL = max_favorable - 0.5R)
     - TP: Fixed at 2.5R
     - Session close: force-close any remaining positions
